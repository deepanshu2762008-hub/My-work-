About Us

Care built around your spine, not a script.

NorthSpire was founded on a simple idea: chiropractic care works best when it's unhurried, precise and genuinely personal. Dr. Konovalov and our care team take the time to understand what's actually driving your pain — then build a plan around your body, your job and your goals, not a one-size-fits-all routine.

  • Treatment plans are re-evaluated every few visits — we adjust the plan, not just your spine.

FAQ

Questions patients ask us most.

Does a chiropractic adjustment hurt?

Most patients feel little to no discomfort — just pressure and a release. Some mild soreness afterward is normal, similar to a light workout, and usually fades within a day.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition, but many patients notice meaningful relief within 4–6 visits. We'll give you a realistic estimate after your first exam — not an open-ended plan.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes — we work with most major insurance providers and will verify your benefits before your first visit so there are no surprises. Flexible payment plans are also available.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Comfortable clothing, any recent imaging or medical records if you have them, and your insurance card. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete your intake form.

Is chiropractic care safe during pregnancy or for children?

Yes. Dr. Konovalov is trained in pregnancy-safe and pediatric-appropriate techniques, which are gentler and adapted to the patient's stage of life.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Often, yes — we hold a few same-week slots for urgent pain. Call or book online and we'll do our best to fit you in quickly.
